# Code of Conduct

MacSpace is intended to be a welcoming and respectful project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
- Give credit to other people's work.
- Help keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized
comments, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through
[@mohindpa](https://github.com/mohindpa) or `hello@websitespa.net`. Reports
will be reviewed as confidentially as practical.
